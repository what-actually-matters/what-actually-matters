Most operators believe they know what their guests experience. They don't. They know what their teams tell them.

Mystery shopping closes that gap. An honest, scored, independent evaluation of the guest journey from arrival to departure, against criteria that actually matters. The truth about what actually happens on a day-to-day basis.

How It Works
Three Stages. One Clear Outcome.
01
The Mystery Visit
An unannounced evaluation conducted as a genuine guest. Every touchpoint assessed: arrival, welcome, staff engagement, cleanliness, safety, F&B, queuing, signage, and departure. Nothing staged. Everything real.
02
The Scored Report
Performance scored against a defined set of criteria across every stage of the guest journey. Clear, honest, benchmarked. You will know exactly where you stand and precisely where the gaps are.
03
The WAM 5 Priorities
From the findings, a WAM 5 is produced. The five things that will have the greatest impact on experience and commercial performance. Not a list of everything that needs fixing. The five that matter most, right now.
WAM  5
What Actually Matters. The WAM 5 that comes from this evaluation is not a generic improvement plan. It is a deliberately prioritised set of five actions, specific to your operation, your guests and your commercial context, that will move the needle if executed with focus and discipline.
What You Receive
Fully scored mystery visit report against defined guest experience criteria
Honest assessment of every touchpoint experienced — nothing softened or qualified
